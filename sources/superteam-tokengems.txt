HOW TO ENTER
1. Open https://tokengems.ai, use Connect Wallet to sign in, and complete your profile.
2. Explore a Solana project through search or the site. Existing token pages and pre-token launch pages are eligible; check that the project is building on Solana.
3. Post a useful take on its TokenGems page. Share a specific observation, thoughtful question, or practical suggestion. If you use a standalone post, tag the project.
4. Follow @tokengems on X (https://x.com/tokengems) and repost the contest announcement (https://x.com/tokengems/status/2104572640199672064). We check both before announcing winners; neither affects your score.
5. Submit your TokenGems profile URL as the main Earn submission link, then add your contribution and project links, plus your X handle, in the questions below.
Already submitted? You are still in. Follow and repost before the deadline, then edit your submission to add your X handle.
One contribution is enough. You may submit your best one to three, each about a different project. We judge your strongest contribution. You can keep using TokenGems normally; three is only the limit on what you submit for judging.
WHAT SHOULD I WRITE?
Tell people what you noticed and why it matters. A few specific sentences can be enough. Explore the page, demo, documentation, or product, and be clear about what you actually tried.
Example of the level of detail: "Your demo lets a merchant accept USDC without displaying a wallet address. That makes checkout easier to follow. Can the merchant issue a partial refund? I could not find that in the demo."
Use your own observation. Thoughtful praise, constructive criticism, and useful questions are equally welcome. Generic comments such as "great project" do not qualify.
